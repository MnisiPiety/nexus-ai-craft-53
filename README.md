# SmartFlow AI

Build a modern, responsive, professional AI Productivity Suite as one integrated web application combining three core AI tools:

Smart Email Generator

Meeting Notes Summarizer

AI Research Assistant

The three tools must feel like parts of the same product rather than separate websites.

Product Concept

Create an elegant AI productivity platform that helps professionals, students, researchers, businesses, and teams write emails, summarize meetings, and conduct research from one dashboard.

The application should have a clean, premium SaaS-style interface with excellent usability on desktop, tablet, and mobile.

Brand & Visual Design

Use a modern technology/productivity aesthetic.

Design requirements:

Clean white/light interface with optional dark mode.

Professional typography.

Generous spacing.

Rounded cards and buttons.

Subtle shadows and borders.

Modern icons.

Smooth hover and transition animations.

Clear visual hierarchy.

Accessible contrast.

Responsive layout.

Professional dashboard experience.

Create a simple AI-inspired logo and brand identity.

Suggested navigation:

Dashboard | Email Generator | Meeting Summarizer | Research Assistant | History | Saved | Settings

Include a prominent New AI Task button.
